🏢 AL MOYASIR (المُيَسِّر)
Professional SaaS Accounting & Commercial Management Software for Moroccan SMEs (TPE/PME)
📌 Executive Summary & Commercial Value
Al Moyasir (المُيَسِّر - The Facilitator) is an enterprise-grade, turn-key accounting and ERP solution tailored specifically for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs / TPE-PME) in Morocco. Built according to the Plan Comptable Général Marocain (PCGM) and Moroccan Tax Authority (DGI / Direction Générale des Impôts) standards, it provides an all-in-one suite covering general accounting, invoicing, cash management, tax declarations (TVA / IS / IR), and financial auditing.

Target Commercial Valuation: 50,000 to 100,000 MAD (Moroccan Dirham) based on full source code acquisition, documentation, and IP transfer rights.

Target Audience: Moroccan accounting firms (Experts-Comptables, Fiduciaires), SMEs, trading companies, services providers, and enterprise software resellers.

✨ Key Features & Business Capabilities
1. 🇲🇦 Moroccan Regulatory & Tax Compliance
PCGM Integration: Full standardized 6-digit Chart of Accounts (Plan Comptable Général Marocain) with pre-loaded Classes 1 through 8.

Value Added Tax (TVA): Automatic calculation for standard rates (20%, 14%, 10%, 7%, 0%), tax deductible/payable accounts, and quarterly/monthly tax declaration preparation.

Corporate & Income Tax: Automated computation schedules for Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) with progressive bracket rates and Cotisation Minimale (CM) baseline calculations.

ICE & Tax Identifiers: Native support for Identifiant Commun de l'Entreprise (ICE), Identifiant Fiscal (IF), Registre du Commerce (RC), and CNSS numbers across all legal invoices and reports.

2. 🧾 Commercial Management & Invoicing
Billing Engine: Multi-currency invoicing (MAD, EUR, USD), pro-forma invoices, sales quotes, and credit notes (Avoirs).

Automated Accounting Entries: Direct conversion of validated sales invoices and purchase bills into double-entry ledger postings (Classes 3, 4, 6, 7).

Payment Tracking: Invoice status workflows (Draft, Validated, Partially Paid, Fully Settled, Overdue) with aging balance reports.

3. ⚖️ Double-Entry Accounting & Ledger Engine
Journal Registers: Sales (Ventes), Purchases (Achats), Bank (Banque), Cash (Caisse), and Miscellaneous (Opérations Diverses / OD).

Real-time Balance Verification: Strict debit/credit balance enforcement preventing unbalanced transaction commits.

General Ledger & Trial Balance: Real-time generation of the Grand Livre and Balance Générale des Comptes.

4. 📊 Financial Statements & Reporting (Bilan & CPC)
Balance Sheet (Bilan): Automated generation of Assets (Actif) and Liabilities/Equity (Passif) according to Moroccan legal formats.

Income Statement (CPC): Compte de Produits et Charges detailing operating, financial, and non-current income and expenses.

Export Options: PDF generation, Excel (XLSX) spreadsheets, and JSON audit trails for tax inspectorate compliance.

5. 🔒 Enterprise Security & Auditability
Immutable Audit Ledger: Non-deletable transaction logs recording all post-validation modifications.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Permissions separation for Administrator, Accountant (Comptable), Cashier (Caissier), and Read-only Auditor roles.

Data Privacy: Full client-side data protection with optional end-to-end database encryption.
